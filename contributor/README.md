# Contributing

**Goal: make Wiki pages to learn Open Source development.**

AlphaClaw is our worked example. [Open the code](https://github.com/PaulTiffany/AlphaClaw). [Open our MIT License](https://github.com/PaulTiffany/AlphaClaw/blob/main/LICENSE). A **license** is a permission slip. MIT lets people use, copy, change, and share AlphaClaw's original work, even for business, if the required notice stays with it.

Other common licenses:
- **Apache 2.0:** like MIT, with extra patent rules.
- **GPL:** shared changed software usually stays under the GPL.
- **CC BY 4.0:** often used for writing and images; you may share and change them if you give credit.

This Wiki is public. Only post work you may share. Do not post passwords, private messages, or personal information.

## 1. Sign in

[![Sign in to GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Sign%20in-GitHub-24292f?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/login?return_to=%2FPaulTiffany%2FAlphaClaw%2Fwiki%2FContributing)

GitHub may ask for your email or username, password, and a security code. When it finishes, it brings you back here.

## 2. Make a page

Look in the **upper-right** for **New Page**. It looks like this:

[![New Page](https://img.shields.io/badge/%2B-New%20Page-1f883d?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/PaulTiffany/AlphaClaw/wiki/_new)

Click it. Add a short title and a few useful sentences.

Gemini may help: **“Make these thoughts into a short Wiki page. Keep my meaning. Do not add facts.”**

Read it. Then click **Save Page**:

![Save Page](https://img.shields.io/badge/Save-Page-1f883d?style=for-the-badge)

Your page is now public, and GitHub keeps its history.

Come back to **Contributing** whenever you want to make another page.
